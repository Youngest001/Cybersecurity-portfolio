# Asset Security and Legal Framework

## 1. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

Legal and regulatory compliance means following laws, regulations and standards that protect data, systems and organizations.

### Key Regulations

- GDPR – Europe
- HIPAA – US healthcare
- PCI-DSS – Payment cards
- NDPA – Nigeria

### Why Compliance Matters

- Avoid financial fines.
- Prevent lawsuits and legal investigations.
- Protect reputation and customer trust.
- Improve security and attract business partners.

## 2. Data Subject, Data Controller and Data Processor

- **Data Subject:** The person whose personal data is collected or processed.
- **Data Controller:** The organization or person who decides how and why personal data is processed.
- **Data Processor:** The person or organization processing personal data on behalf of a controller.

## 3. Key Regulations

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation – Europe)

- Applies to businesses processing the personal data of people in the EU, including businesses outside Europe in certain circumstances.
- Focus: Data privacy and protection.
- Individuals have the right to access their personal data.
- Reportable breaches generally must be reported within 72 hours.
- Penalties : €20 million or 4% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher.

### HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act – US)

- Applies to healthcare providers, insurers and other covered entities and business associates handling protected health information.
- Focus: Protecting PHI (Protected Health Information).
- Security Rule: Protects health information through security measures and access controls.
- Breach Notification Rule: Requires notification following certain breaches.
- Penalties : up to $1.5 million per year per violation type.

### PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)

- Applies to organizations that store, process or transmit payment card data.
- Focus: Preventing payment card fraud and protecting cardholder information.
- Encrypt cardholder data.
- Maintain firewalls.
- Conduct regular vulnerability testing.
- Restrict access to cardholder data.

### NDPA (Nigeria Data Protection Act)

- Nigeria's data protection law.
- Focus: Protecting personal data and regulating its processing.
- Organizations must comply with data protection requirements.
- Penalties :10 million or 2% of annual turnover.

## 4. Why Compliance Matters

- **Avoiding Fines:** Regulations can impose significant financial penalties.
- **Legal Protection:** Lawsuits and investigations can drain company resources.
- **Reputation and Trust:** Customers may lose trust in organizations that expose their data.
- **Competitive Advantage:** Compliance can attract clients, customers and business partners.
