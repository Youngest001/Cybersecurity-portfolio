OSINT Basics

OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) is the process of gathering publicly available information about a person or organization.

Types of OSINT

Passive OSINT: Gathering publicly available information without directly interacting with the target.

Active OSINT: Sending traffic to a target to observe how it responds.

Tools Learnt

WHOIS: Used to find domain registration information, registration dates, registrant details and nameservers.

Wayback Machine: Used to view older versions of websites.

crt.sh: Used for subdomain discovery.

WhatsMyName: Used to check where a username exists online.

Ethical Rules

Focus on an organization's public footprint.
Don't profile private individuals.
Perform passive research only.
Don't attempt to obtain passwords or scan targets.
Record findings, dates and evidence.
A match is a lead, not proof.
