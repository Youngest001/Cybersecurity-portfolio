 DAY 1: INTRODUCTION TO CYBERSECURITY

 1. What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, applications, and data from unauthorized access, cyberattacks, damage, or theft.

Its main purpose is to protect information and ensure that digital systems remain safe and secure.

2. The CIA Triad

The CIA Triad : CIA triad is a conceptual framework that ensure system protect data, maintaining the data and available when needed. It's fundamental cybersecurity model consisting of three main principles:

A. Confidentiality
Confidentiality means ensuring that information is only accessible to authorized people.
Example: Using a password to prevent unauthorized people from accessing your bank account.

 B. Integrity
Integrity means ensuring that information remains accurate, complete, and is not changed without authorization.
Example: Preventing a hacker from secretly changing a customer's bank account balance.

 C. Availability
Availability means ensuring that information and systems are accessible whenever authorized users need them.
Example: Ensuring that a bank's mobile application remains available for customers to access their accounts.

 3. NIST Cybersecurity Framework: NIST Framework is a US standard guide that protects organization from cybersecurity risk.
NIST stands for National Institute of Standards and Technology.
The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides guidelines that help organizations understand, manage, and reduce cybersecurity risks.
It consists of six main functions:

 1. Identify
Identify means understanding the organization's assets, vulnerabilities, threats, and potential cybersecurity risks.
Example: A company identifies its computers, servers, and sensitive customer information.

 2. Protect
Protect means implementing security measures to safeguard systems, networks, and information against cyber threats.

**Example:** Using passwords, firewalls, antivirus software, and encryption.

### 3. Detect
Detect means identifying cybersecurity threats, suspicious activities, and security incidents.

Example: Detecting unusual login attempts or malware activities on a network.

 4. Respond
Respond means taking appropriate action when a cybersecurity incident occurs.

**Example:** Disconnecting an infected computer from the network and investigating the attack.

### 5. Recover
Recover means restoring affected systems, data, and services after a cybersecurity incident.

**Example:** Restoring lost company files using backup copies after a cyberattack.

### 6. Govern
Govern means establishing cybersecurity policies, responsibilities, rules, and strategies to manage cybersecurity risks.

**Example:** A company creates security policies that employees must follow to protect company information.

## 4. Summary

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting digital systems, networks, and information from cyber threats.

The CIA Triad provides three fundamental security principles:

- Confidentiality – Keeping information private.
- Integrity – Keeping information accurate.
- Availability – Keeping information accessible.

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides six functions:

1. Identify
2. Protect
3. Detect
4. Respond
5. Recover
6. Govern

These concepts form an important foundation for understanding cybersecurity.
